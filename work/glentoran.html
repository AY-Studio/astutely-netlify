01 · The club

The pride of East Belfast.

Founded in 1882 and at The Oval since 1892, under the Harland and Wolff cranes, Glentoran is one of the most decorated clubs in Irish football, and one of only three never to have been relegated from the top division.

Today it is more than a first team. There is a women's team, a growing academy, a social partnership that has engaged more than 3,500 people, a superstore, its own streaming channel and a commercial programme with partners across Northern Ireland. That is a lot of stories, for a lot of audiences, every single week.

  • 1882founded in East Belfast
  • 23Irish League titles
  • 23Irish Cups
  • 1914first British club to win a European trophy, the Vienna Cup

02 · The challenge

A big club, spread thin online.

In early 2025, 81% of visitors to glentoran.com arrived on a phone, but the site was built for desktop. The menu sprawled, the online shop ran on a separate platform, and ticketing sat outside the website.

Before After

  • Built for the wrong screen

    Four out of five fans were on mobile, scrolling a desktop layout with a crowded menu.

  • Disconnected systems

    The online shop ran on its own store platform and ticketing lived outside the website, so fans hopped between tools to buy anything.

  • A sprawling structure

    Matches, teams, commercial, community, history and fans, with dozens of pages competing for attention and no clear first step.

04 · Campaigns & comms

#StrongerTogether: more than a slogan.

For the 2025/26 close season we built one overarching campaign to carry season tickets, both kit launches and the new training wear: #StrongerTogether, a rallying cry about loyalty, unity and East Belfast pride.

It was built to run across email, social, video, the website and digital screens, with the club's own fans and players at the centre of every piece.

  1. Loyalty first

    Existing season ticket holders got priority renewal, through a three-part email and social series: a tease, an offer and a deadline.

  2. General sale

    Then the doors opened to everyone, with a dedicated season ticket hub, instalment plans and a clear FAQ.

  3. One look, everywhere

    The same creative ran on email, social, video, the website and roadside screens, so every touchpoint reinforced the last.

05 · Design

A matchday identity the club can run itself.

We built the club a 60-template social system for 2025/26: news, statements, signings, starting XIs, kick-off to full time, player of the match, tickets, the superstore and the community, all in one confident, consistent look.

It is designed so the club's media team can turn matchday posts around fast, and every one still looks unmistakably Glentoran.

Selling the club, on paper too.

From the 2027 to 2030 primary partner brochure to the Hall of Fame dinner and its limited edition jersey, we give the commercial team materials that look as ambitious as the club's plans.
