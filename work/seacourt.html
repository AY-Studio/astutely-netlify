01 · The client

An open door to the magic of print

Seacourt began in 1981 in the basement of a teachers' centre, started by two printmakers who wanted to practise close to home. Four decades on it's an open-access workshop, artists' studios, an education room and Bangor's first professional gallery.

In 2023 Seacourt realised a long-held dream: a home in the heart of the town, inside the grand old Ulster Bank building. A landmark building deserved a brand, and a website, with the same presence.

02 · The challenge

Remarkable work. Hard to see.

Seacourt had decades of beautiful prints, photography and stories. Online, they sat behind a busy layout, a slow video header and a stamp-like logo that couldn't stretch to everything Seacourt had become.

  • A new home to announce

03 · The brand

Evolved, not erased

Forty years of goodwill lived in that ink-stamped roundel, so we didn't throw it away. We kept its hand-printed texture and condensed lettering, then freed the name from the circle so it could stand on its own.

The result is a bold wordmark, a split-circle "S" mark drawn from the original stamp, and a naming system that stretches from the Print Workshop to Seacourt Studios, the Gallery and the Seacourt Circle of supporters.

  2. Refined A stronger stamp
