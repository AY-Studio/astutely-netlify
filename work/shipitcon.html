01 · The conference

Built by the community, for the community.

Every September, engineers, leaders and students fill the Round Room at Dublin's Mansion House for a single day of talks about how great software gets shipped.

It's run by volunteers and funded by sponsors. Leftover funds go to local good causes, and the team works hard to make the day accessible and inclusive, from financial assistance tickets to a detailed venue accessibility guide.

Every talk connects to a coherent narrative that makes the whole day feel like a journey rather than a collection of disparate presentations.

Declan Boylan, Teamwork.com, attendee testimonial on shipitcon.com

02 · The challenge

A volunteer team. One day. A room to fill.

Each edition starts from scratch: a new theme to bring to life, speakers to announce, sponsors to win and tickets to sell, all by a team with day jobs.

  • 01A new story every year

    Each edition has its own theme. It needs a look, a voice and a campaign that builds excitement over months, not days.

  • 02Sponsors make it possible

    As a not-for-profit, ShipItCon relies on sponsors. They need a clear, confident case for getting involved.

  • 03Outgrowing a blog

    Until 2023 the website ran on an off-the-shelf blog theme. It couldn't carry a new theme each year or keep past editions to hand.

03 · Our approach

The theme comes first. Everything else follows it.

We first joined ShipItCon as a partner in 2022. Since then we've treated each edition like a launch: once the team picks the theme, we turn it into a world that runs from the first teaser to the big screens on the day, and the website carries it all year.

04 · #SIC2023

The Unknown: a mission to deep space.

2023's theme explored risk, uncertainty and the things you can't predict. We built a cinematic, sci-fi world around a lone astronaut, and rolled the campaign out like a mission briefing.

The payoff was the keynote: Dr Norah Patten, Ireland's first astronaut in training.

  1. The teaser

    "Transmission incoming." A dark, quiet start that got people guessing.

  2. The reveal

    The theme, the date and the look, launched across social, print and the website.

  3. The keynote

    "Confirmed for launch." Each big announcement landed in the same world.

05 · #SIC2024

Flow: from the stage to the sponsor pack.

2024 was about the currents of software delivery. We gave it a dot-matrix identity and streams of glowing particles, designed to move on the big screens in the Round Room.

The same world shaped the 2024 sponsor prospectus, which made the case for partnering with a not-for-profit event.
