Case study · Budget Energy SuperCupNI

The 43rd SuperCupNI. The first with an app.

In 2026 we delivered a connected digital and PR campaign for one of the world's leading youth football tournaments. A new website, its first iOS and Android apps and the platform behind them, launched and amplified through PR, media, photography, editorial, email and social. On the first day of matches, the app was No.6 in the UK App Store's Top Free Sports chart.

01 · The client

One of the biggest weeks in world youth football.

Since 1983, first as the Milk Cup, the tournament has brought elite academy and representative teams to Northern Ireland every July. Kobbie Mainoo, Kyle Walker and Darren Fletcher all played in it on their way to the top.

Matches are spread across Causeway Coast and Glens, Antrim and Newtownabbey, and Mid and East Antrim, with every game streamed live and teams, families and scouts travelling from four continents.

  • 60,000+fans through the gates
  • 1,200+young players
  • 17venues across three council areas
  • £11.19mdirect economic benefit

02 · The challenge

Get people talking.
Then give them one place to follow it all.

With more than 150 fixtures in five days across three council areas, fans, families and teams needed fast, reliable answers: who's playing next, where, how the groups stand and how to get tickets. At the same time, the tournament needed to reach new audiences, sell tickets and deliver for its partners. A new app on its own wouldn't do that. It needed a campaign behind it.

04 · The platform

One platform behind every screen.

We built a single tournament platform: an API and content management system holding every team, fixture, venue and result.

The tournament team enters information once and it updates the website, the iOS app, the Android app and the match board together. One workflow for a small team under pressure, and one version of the truth for fans.

05 · The campaign

Building momentum before a ball was kicked.

From the spring trophy shoot to the launch photocall in July, we planned and produced the moments that kept the tournament in the news, and used every one of them to send people to the new website and app.

The website and app were officially launched on 22 July 2026, four days before the opening parade. The project was funded through the National Lottery Dormant Assets for All Fund.

  1. PR & press

    Press releases and media relations around every milestone, including the digital launch, which was covered by The Irish News.

  2. Media management

    Accreditation, briefings and a simple way for photographers to deliver their images during tournament week.

  3. Photoshoots

    Planned shoots for launches and announcements, giving press, partners and social a steady supply of strong images.

  4. Articles & news

    Stories on supercupni.com that kept the website fresh and gave fans a reason to come back.

  5. Email

    Campaigns to fans, media and partners, from ticket news to tournament week updates.

  6. Social

    Content for the tournament's channels through the build-up and tournament week.

06 · Results: day one

No.6

No.6 in the UK App Store on the first day of matches.

By the end of the first day of matches, SuperCupNI was the sixth most downloaded free sports app in the UK, behind only Playtomic, the Premier League, Spond, The Hundred and the Official England App.

It ranked ahead of the Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games, Sky Sports, DAZN, the Official Liverpool FC App and TNT Sports.
