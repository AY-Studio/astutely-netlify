Case study · Brain Injury Matters

A warmer front door for everyone affected by brain injury.

We redesigned the Brain Injury Matters website around the people it exists for, so anyone arriving at a hard moment can find their way to the right support.

Brain Injury Matters supports children, adults, families and professionals across Northern Ireland. Their new website leads with who you are and what you need, speaks in plain, reassuring language, and makes the next step, whether that's a referral, a chat or a donation, easy to take.

01 · The charity

Life after brain injury, lived together.

An acquired brain injury can come from an accident, a stroke, an illness or an assault. In an instant it changes a person's world, and the world of everyone around them.

Brain Injury Matters walks that road with them: early support for families, specialist services for children and young people, and long-term community programmes for adults, like Pedal Power, The Studio and their choir.

  • 100%of families would recommend the children's and youth service to others
  • 203children, young people and family members supported in a year
  • 2,330adult arts, sport, wellbeing and counselling sessions

Source: Brain Injury Matters Impact Report 2022/23

02 · The challenge

People arrive at the hardest moment of their lives.

Often it's a parent sitting in a hospital corridor, or someone months into recovery and wondering what comes next. The old website was organised around the charity, not around them. It was hard to know where to start, and harder to take the first step.

Before After

Drag to compare the previous homepage with the new one.

  • Organised around the charity

    Menus like About, Information and Get Help asked visitors to know how the charity worked before they could find help.

  • No clear route to referral

    There was no simple, guided way to ask for support online, or to know what would happen next.

  • Hard to keep fresh

    Events and news fell out of date, so the warmth of the community never came through online.

03 · Our approach

Start with people. Then make every next step easy.

Through discovery workshops with the team, we mapped who comes to the site, what they're feeling and what they need to do. Four principles shaped everything that followed.

  • Lead with who you are

    Navigation starts with Who We Help and How We Help, with tailored journeys for children, adults, parents and carers, and professionals.

  • Warm and open

    A soft cream ground, rounded shapes, real faces from the community and plain, reassuring language. Never clinical, never cold.

  • One clear next step

    Referral and Donate sit in the header of every page, and every page ends with a single, obvious way forward.

  • Easy to look after

    A WordPress build the team can update themselves, so events, stories and news stay as current as the community.

04 · Design

A design system with a soft heart.

We built on the charity's colourful identity: navy for trust, green for growth, and bright yellow and pink lifted straight from the logo. Photos sit in playful offset frames, sections curve into one another, and Montserrat keeps everything clear and friendly.

Every page was designed in Figma before a line of code, from the homepage and audience journeys to Brain Injuries Explained and the referral form.

05 · Referrals

Asking for help, made simple.

We digitised the referral process. Families, professionals and people with a brain injury can now refer online in minutes, and see exactly what happens next.

  1. You make the first move

    Send the referral form or pick up the phone.

  2. Acknowledge

    The team gets in contact to acknowledge your referral.

  3. Review

    They review the referral and get in touch with any questions.

  4. Assessment

    One of the team arranges a first conversation.

07 · What we delivered

Built to support people, and the team behind them.

  • Four audience journeys

    Tailored paths for children and young people, adults, parents and carers, and professionals.

  • "Not sure where to look?"

    A simple "I am…" selector that takes anyone straight to the right place.

  • Online referrals

    A guided online referral form and a clear four-step process, from first contact to first conversation.

  • Brain injuries explained

    Plain-language information on what an acquired brain injury is and what recovery looks like.

  • A living community

    Events, stories, campaigns, Pedal Power and The Studio, all easy for the team to update.

  • Ways to give

    Donate, fundraise, volunteer and partner journeys, one tap from anywhere on the site.

  • Accessibility built in

    An on-page accessibility toolbar, clear type and generous spacing throughout.

  • Made to last

    A custom WordPress build with newsletter sign-up, analytics and full team training.

08 · The community

Real people. Real belonging.

The new site puts the charity's own community front and centre, because nothing says "you are not alone" like seeing the people who've walked the road before you.
