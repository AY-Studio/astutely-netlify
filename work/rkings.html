01 · The client

A business built on trust and timing.

R Kings sells entries into prize draws, from cars and cash to holidays and tech, to players across the UK and Ireland. Every competition has a fixed number of entries and a draw date, and every draw is streamed live.

In a market full of lookalike sites, two things decide who players choose: whether they trust you, and how easy you make it to take part. Everything we have done together starts from those two questions.

02 · The plan

We started with a plan, not a website.

Our first session with R Kings, in January 2021, set one goal: improve the customer experience, increase dwell time and ultimately increase sales. We broke that into six things to build, and they have guided the work ever since.

  • 01

    The competition experience

    More ways in: by price, category, time to draw and what's featured, so every player finds something for them.

  • 02

    The brand

    A consistent look, voice and standard of imagery that earns a premium place in a crowded market.

  • 03

    Trust

    Winners, live draws, entry lists and charity giving, shown openly, so players can see it's real.

  • 04

    Engagement

    A tone of voice and mix of prizes that speaks to many different kinds of player.

  • 05

    Loyalty

    Reasons to come back: more chances to win and messages that arrive at the right moment.

  • 06

    Awareness

    Content worth sharing, planned around the prizes and moments that matter.

03 · The platform

Built for the moment someone decides to enter.

We redesigned the site around the decisions players make: what's on, how long is left, how many entries remain and how much it costs. Today, countdown timers, progress bars and clear prices sit on every competition, with one obvious way to enter.

In early 2022 we redesigned the site in Figma, then reviewed the new build page by page, from the homepage to the basket, order confirmation and 404 page, until it matched.

04 · Brand & content

A system the whole team can run every week.

A great platform only works if what goes on it is consistently good. So we wrote the rules: the Digital Experience Guide, an 83-page manual covering brand standards, content planning, photography, copy, email, social and SMS, and a Content Capture Guide for every prize shoot.

They turned one-off efforts into a repeatable weekly rhythm.

  1. Review

    Every Monday, look back at how last week's competitions and marketing performed.

  2. Check

    Confirm this week's emails, posts and messages are created and scheduled.

  3. Plan

    Line up the content needed for next week's prizes.

  4. Look ahead

    Flag the big prizes and moments coming beyond that.

The guide gives every prize its own code, so it can be tracked from shoot to draw:
