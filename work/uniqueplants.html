01 · The client

A landscape architect who started with plants.

Andrew studied horticulture, then landscape architecture, then spent ten years in London designing high-end gardens in France, Spain and London and luxury resorts in Mallorca, Mauritius, Dubai, Portugal and Malta.

Unique Plants brings that experience home. It is a specialist soft landscape and garden design consultancy, based at The Gatehouse in Warrenpoint and in London, working with private and commercial clients across the UK and Ireland.

02 · The brief

A new practice that needed to look as good as its gardens.

Andrew's clients commission gardens that will stand the test of time. The brand had to signal that same level of quality from the first glance, without shouting. We agreed five words at the start, and every decision was measured against them.

03 · The identity

Two words, growing together.

A classic serif for Unique, a clean sans for Plants. Subtly different, like the plants Andrew brings together, but they simply belong side by side.

The wordmark pairs Untitled Serif with Basis Grotesque. On the website, the same contrast carries through in a Garamond serif and Inter, so the brand reads the same wherever clients meet it.

The palette starts with cream, deep green and sage, and adds brighter notes, from lime to burgundy, for moments that need to bloom.

The monogram
  • A spade,
  • Roots,
  • Seedling,
  • The first leaf,
  • A flag in the ground.

The U and P interlock into a single mark that reads as all five, a small symbol for a practice putting down roots.

04 · In the world

Made for site hoardings as much as screens.

Landscape projects happen in public. We showed how the brand would hold up on a construction site, on workwear and on the signs that mark a project from the road.

Signage in three sizes, from plaque to site board.

In 2024 we followed up with signage: a small 15 by 30cm plaque, a 30 by 60cm sign and a large 60 by 90cm board, including a design and build version shared with Annett Landscaping.

05 · The website

Like stepping into a garden.

Full-screen imagery that moves gently, generous space and a slow, editorial rhythm. The website explains a specialist service in plain language, then makes booking a meeting the natural next step.

06 · What we delivered

One consistent brand, from the first meeting to the site board.

  • Website

    A four-page site that introduces the practice, tells Andrew's story and explains each service, with a booking invitation on every page.

  • Built to be run in-house

    A Squarespace build Andrew can keep up to date himself, with a contact form and both office locations.

  • Creative direction

    A clear brief in five words, references and a direction agreed before design began.

  • Wordmark and monogram

    A serif and sans wordmark and an interlocking UP monogram that works at any size.

  • Colour and type

    A nine-colour palette and a type system that carry from print to screen.

  • Signage

    Plaque, sign and site board designs in three sizes, including a shared design and build board.
